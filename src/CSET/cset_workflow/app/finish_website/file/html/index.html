How to use this page

Choose which fields you're interested in and what kind of diagnostics to show, then press Go to open the diagnostics viewer with a ready-made filter.

  • Ticking several fields shows diagnostics from any of them.
  • The Show option then narrows those down to aggregated or non-aggregated diagnostics.
  • With no fields ticked, the Show option applies to every diagnostic.
  • Once in the viewer you can refine the filter by typing in the search box.

Filter syntax

Each condition has the form facet operator value, for example:

field:temperature
"temperature" appears in the field name.
field=x_wind OR field=y_wind
Field is exactly "x_wind" or "y_wind".
histogram AND NOT aggregated
Histograms that aren't aggregated (no facet means "search the title").

Full details are under Filter help in the viewer sidebar.
